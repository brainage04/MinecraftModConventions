# Release notes

**3.5.0 adds the [recording rules](CLIENT_GAMETESTS.md#shooting-a-recording).** `showStep` and `showStepAndHold` fail a caption that says what was removed or changed, so a mod whose captions do that fails its client GameTests until it rewords them. Recordings show every particle (`particles:0`), and `GTR_RECORDING_FPS=60` records the video at 60 frames a second on a card display, where the client's frame cap follows the capture rate; a client that cannot draw 60 presents fewer, and the measured 60 fps take presented 43.6 frames a second and repeated 997 of its 2816 video frames. A recording now captures the game's own picture and sound (`GTR_RECORDING_CAPTURE=game`, see [Client GameTest recording](RECORDING.md#client-gametest-recording)): the client copies each finished frame out of its render target and plays its sound into an OpenAL loopback device, so every video frame is one whole picture, none can tear, and the machine plays no sound. `GTR_RECORDING_CAPTURE=x11grab` grabs the screen as before, and on a card display such a take replaces the frames x11grab tears with the frame before each. It also replaces a frame where the screen changed below a line one capture before it changed above it, such as a chat bar opening whose suggestion list appears above it a capture later, which repeats the previous picture for one frame. On a 25-minute TwitchPlaysMinecraft take it replaced 134 of 46,156 frames, tears and such frames together. A tear on a frame where the caption or another overlay also changes can go unrepaired and unreported. New checks can fail a recording: an x11grab card take that still has a torn frame after that repair fails unless `GTR_RECORDING_TEAR_CHECK=warn`, a `GTR_RECORDING_FPS` above 30 that gets the virtual display fails before the client starts, an encoder that cannot encode a test frame fails before the client starts, and a game take fails on a graphics backend other than OpenGL, on an OpenAL without `ALC_SOFT_loopback`, or when its capture stops mid-take; `GTR_RECORDING_CAPTURE=x11grab` is the fallback for the last three. Every recording whose mod keeps MinecraftModConventions' caption panel shows a [performance overlay](CLIENT_GAMETESTS.md#shooting-a-recording) under it, which a test can move or hide with `ClientGameTestRecorder.placePerformanceOverlay`, and keeps its numbers as `perf*` values in the metadata. `GTR_RECORDING_VIDEO_ENCODER=h264_nvenc` encodes the capture on an NVIDIA card, and `GTR_RECORDING_TRACE=true` writes the time of every present beside the boundaries. Self-hosted runners now use SDL's EGL path too, which Minecraft 26.3 clients need there, so a self-hosted runner needs EGL installed (`libegl1` and `libegl-mesa0`).

**3.4.0 makes the strict quality policy the default.** `check` and `build` now fail on unformatted code (Spotless with google-java-format AOSP), on Checkstyle findings, on deprecation, unchecked and rawtypes warnings, and on the [deprecated GameTest API check](QUALITY_CHECKS.md#deprecated-gametest-api-check). A consumer that cannot take all of that in one go passes `-PstrictQuality=false`, which turns the whole policy off and leaves the three lints as warnings, or `-PstrictUnchecked=false`, which keeps formatting, Checkstyle and deprecation as errors and turns the unchecked and rawtypes lints off, so nothing is reported for them. The reusable workflows set neither property; the route that reaches every job is the project's `gradle.properties` (`strictQuality=false` or `strictUnchecked=false`), which the plugin reads on every Gradle invocation, including the release-preparation and client GameTest jobs. The `gradle_task` input, which carries Gradle tasks and arguments, exists in `reusable-mod-build.yml`, `reusable-production-gametests.yml` and `reusable-neoforge-gametests.yml` only, so it does not cover the release-preparation or client GameTest jobs by itself (the Modrinth and CurseForge workflows take a `gradle_task` that names their publish task). A project whose `gametest` source set exists also gets the film-only [recording source set](CLIENT_GAMETESTS.md#film-only-code-in-the-recording-source-set) (`src/recording/java` and `src/recording/resources`), which is empty until used, compiles against everything a GameTest compiles against including the mod's own main and client classes, and is packaged into the production GameTest JAR.
